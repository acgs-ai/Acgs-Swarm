# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, constructive, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and professional.
- Give actionable, evidence-based feedback.
- Assume good intent, and ask clarifying questions before escalating.
- Keep technical discussions focused on code, design, and reproducibility.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Personal attacks, insults, or trolling.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Disruptive behavior that prevents productive collaboration.

## Scope
This code of conduct applies in repository spaces (issues, PRs, discussions, reviews) and project-run communication channels.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions/comments that violate this policy.

For severe or repeated incidents, maintainers may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

## Reporting
Report conduct concerns to `hello@acgs.dev` with relevant context (links, timestamps, screenshots).

Security vulnerabilities should be reported via [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), not through conduct channels.
