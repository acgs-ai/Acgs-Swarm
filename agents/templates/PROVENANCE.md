## What was vendored

The persona-agent category directories and orchestration docs (`academic`,
`design`, `engineering`, `examples`, `finance`, `game-development`, `marketing`,
`paid-media`, `product`, `project-management`, `sales`, `spatial-computing`,
`specialized`, `strategy`, `support`, `testing`).

## What was intentionally left out

- `integrations/` — placeholder READMEs for tool-format files generated by the
  upstream `convert.sh`; nothing to vendor.
- `scripts/` — upstream install/convert/lint machinery, specific to the source
  repo's release flow.
- `.github/`, root `README.md`, `CONTRIBUTING*`, `SECURITY.md` — upstream repo
  metadata, not template content.

## Updating

To refresh against upstream, re-clone the source repo, re-copy the category
directories over `agents/templates/`, and regenerate [`INVENTORY.md`](INVENTORY.md).
Bump the commit hash and date in this file.
